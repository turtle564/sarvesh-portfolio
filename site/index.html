What racing taught me

Habits that carry over

Precision

Races are won and lost in hundredths of a second. Every braking point, steering input and throttle application has to be exact. That zero-margin discipline carries into strategy, analysis and execution.

Decisions under pressure

Tyre management, gaps and overtake windows are decided in seconds, with no room for hesitation. Making fast, accurate calls under stress is an edge in any role.

Teamwork

No driver wins alone. Engineering, mechanics, strategy and data all count. I give clear technical feedback and build trust with the people around me.

Dedication

Racing at a competitive level while completing a degree takes structured commitment and time management, in the kart and in the classroom.
